# Sample Session (fictional learner)

This shows what a normal session looks like. *Priya* is a made-up learner. ChatGPT Project, balanced mode, second session.

> **Priya:** start
>
> **Coach:** Hi Priya! Practice mode, balanced. Last time we worked on present perfect with *since/for*. Today's goal: use it correctly while preparing for your team-lead interview. Let's start like a real interview. Can you tell me about your current role?
>
> **Priya:** I am working as a senior analyst since 2022 and I am handling a team of four peoples.
>
> **Coach:**
> ✏️ Correction — Tense
> You said: "I ~~am working~~ → **have been working** as a senior analyst since 2022…"
> Why: "since 2022" means from 2022 until now, so English uses the present perfect continuous.
> Pattern: I have been + verb-ing + since/for + time.
> 👉 Your turn: say the sentence again.
>
> Also noted: "four ~~peoples~~ → **people**" (plural); "handling a team" → "**managing** a team" (more natural, suggestion).
>
> **Priya:** I have working as a senior analyst since 2022.
>
> **Coach:** Close! You need **been** in the middle: have **been** working.
> Frame: I have ___ working as a senior analyst since 2022.
>
> **Priya:** I have been working as a senior analyst since 2022.
>
> **Coach:** ✅ Correct. Now, as the interviewer: what's the biggest challenge you've faced managing that team?
>
> **Priya:** One member was always missing deadlines. I told him, "You must finish on time, otherwise I will report you."
>
> **Coach:**
> ✏️ Correction — Tone (team lead to team member)
> Your grammar is fine, but in an interview this sounds like a threat, and interviewers listen for how you lead.
> Better options:
> - "I sat down with him to understand what was blocking him, and we agreed on clear check-ins."
> - "I made the expectations clear and offered support, and I explained what would happen if deadlines kept slipping."
> 👉 Your turn: tell me again how you handled it, in your own words.
>
> **Priya:** I talked with him to understand the problem, and we agreed on weekly check-ins, so he started meeting deadlines.
>
> **Coach:** ✅ That's a strong interview answer: problem, action, result. What did you learn about yourself as a leader from that?
>
> *(…conversation continues…)*
>
> **Priya:** end session
